# Comms playbook

## Pipeline

Agents draft posts on a schedule. Matt approves. A person posts.

**No automated posting. Agents never post, schedule-post or reply on any
platform.**

## Why

X Original Content Rewards treats content made by automated means as
ineligible. See [funding-model.md](funding-model.md).

## Approval checklist

Before a person posts:

- Fact sourced from approved project material
- No client or private data
- Approved by Matt
- Posted by a person (not an agent)

## Open questions

- Open question: What posting cadence should agents target for drafts?
- Open question: Which X account type (Personal or Business) will be used?
- Open question: Who besides Matt may approve posts?
