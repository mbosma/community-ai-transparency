## Core workflow

Local governments request a review by the AI for any change, with publicly
accessible results, and findings are shared automatically with constituents.
The aim is to bring light to the dark corners of bills, where corruption
happens. Start local and expand until it forces federal change.

Open question: Which local government requests the first review?
