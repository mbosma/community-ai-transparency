# Legal structure research

**Not legal advice. Research outline and open questions only.**

## Outline

- **501(c)(3):** charitable/educational arm.
- **501(c)(4):** unlimited lobbying, limited campaign activity.
- **Wide political capabilities:** a 501(c)(4) for lobbying and limited
  campaign activity, with a linked 501(c)(3) for tax-deductible education and
  research, and activities kept segregated.
