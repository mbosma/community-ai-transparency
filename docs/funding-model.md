# Funding model

## The loop

An X account earns view payouts; payouts buy more compute; community traction
unlocks more compute.

## Compute kickstart

Kickstart with unused subscription quota (Matt's $200 Cursor plan) spent on
project agents, tracked in [compute-ledger.md](compute-ledger.md). Cursor has
no token-transfer feature.

## X Original Content Rewards constraints

- Premium required (paid, so it is Matt's call)
- 500 verified followers
- 500K verified Home Timeline impressions in 90 days (replies excluded)
- content made by automated means is ineligible
- political or government organisation accounts are ineligible

Therefore agents draft and a person posts, from a Personal or Business account.

Checked 10/19; Creator Revenue Sharing retired 9/7/2026. Sources:
help.x.com/en/using-x/original-content-rewards (via blotato and usefastlane
recaps). Re-check before relying on it.
