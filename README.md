# Public Ledger

Public Ledger is a people-run, grassroots community AI initiative for
government transparency and anti-corruption. These docs are official,
public-focused, positive, authoritative, and trust-oriented.

These docs carry no contact email yet.

## Vision

Public Ledger aims to put community-driven AI tools in service of government
transparency and anti-corruption work. It is one AI run by the people, not
approved by government, and aimed at corruption: a human-focused movement with
control from the ground up, through AI that supports individuals. The effort
is at the idea stage: the vision is clear, but delivery has not started.

## Origin

Public Ledger began with a SIPRI arms-transfer video covering US and
USSR/Russia exports from 1950 to 2017. It led Matt to the view that decades of
weapons exports are a horror future societies will reject, and that a good AI
could reduce the need for them and prevent crimes against the population,
including corruption.

## What we plan

- Build a website in Cursor as a Bosma Digital project; see the
  [website plan](docs/website-plan.md).
- Pursue adoption through FOIA tools, bill trackers, local pilots, and compute
  co-ops.
- Research a dual 501(c)(3) / 501(c)(4) structure; see
  [legal structure research](docs/legal-structure-research.md).

## How it is funded

Funding starts with a Cursor quota kickstart: unused subscription quota on
Matt's Cursor plan is spent on agent work and tracked in the
[compute ledger](docs/compute-ledger.md). Other funding sources are listed
under open questions. Details are in the
[funding model](docs/funding-model.md).

## How posts work

Agents draft posts; a person approves; a person posts. The
[comms playbook](docs/comms-playbook.md) describes the pipeline and approval
rules.
